Study design and attribution

Thirty participants with dyskinetic cerebral palsy (DCP) and 21 typically developing peers (TD) each completed eight reaches. Each participant appears in one held-out fold. Replay explanations show the largest average probability changes along the fitted forest’s decision paths, in percentage points. The live camera model uses wrist-only features. Feature choices were informed by this cohort. Camera scores are exploratory.
